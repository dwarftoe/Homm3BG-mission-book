\addscenariosection{1}{Cooperative Scenario}{Underground Attack}{\images/underground_attack.png}

\begin{multicols*}{2}

\textbf{Author:} dwarftoe

\textit{The earth is shaking! Oh no ... it has begun here as well. Refugees from a distant kingdom had spoken of terrible earthquakes that had devastated their entire homeland. Whole regions had sunk into the trembling earth. Mountains had cracked, forests had collapsed, and the earth itself had been torn apart. Then, without warning, the ground split open in front of the cities. From the depths beneath the world, hordes of monstrous creatures poured forth. Like a living tide, they swept across the land, setting everything ablaze and storming the cities. No army had been prepared for such an enemy. No wall could withstand them. Kingdom after kingdom fell into ruin. Only a handful of people survived the devastation. They fled as far as they could, carrying with them the horrific tales of what they had witnessed. But now ... the same thing is happening here. The earth trembles beneath people's feet. Cracks are spreading across the ground. Something is stirring far below the surface. The kingdoms have little time to act. For the first time, their rulers set aside their ancient rivalries and forge a desperate alliance. Their armies gather, their gates are sealed, and their warriors prepare to face the unknown horror rising from the depths of the earth. But there is one terrible question that no one can answer: How do you fight an enemy whose attack you cannot see coming? The ground shakes again. Something is coming. Something ancient. Something hungry. Brace yourselves for the end.}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Scenario Length}} 5 + $2P$ Rounds ($P$ stands for the number of players)

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Player Setup}}
\textbf{Player Count:} 1--5

\textbf{Starting Resources:} 13 \svg{gold}, 4 \svg{building_materials}, 1 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Income:} 10 \svg{gold}, 0 \svg{building_materials}, 0 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Units:}
\begin{itemize}
\item A ``Pack'' of the cheapest \svg{bronze} Units
\item ``Few'' of the most expensive \svg{bronze} Units
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Town Buildings:} \svg{bronze}~Dwelling, City Hall, \svg[12]{building_special_gears}~Building

\textbf{Map Tile Pool:} Each player takes 1 random Far (II--III) Tile

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Map Setup}} Take the following Tiles and arrange them as shown in the Scenario map layout ($P$ stands for the number of players):

\begin{itemize}
\item $P$ × Starting (I) Tile
\item 1 × Starting (I) Dungeon Tile
\item $2P$ × Far (II--III) Tiles
\item $2P$ × Near (IV--V) Underground Tiles
\item 1--3 × Center (VI--VII) Underground Tiles (depending on $P$)
\item 1--2 × Near (IV--V) Underground Tiles (depending on $P$)
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Victory Conditions}} You must venture underground and defeat the evil Warmage in his town.
\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Defeat Conditions}} The players lose at the end of round 5 + $2P$. If a town is captured by the enemy AI army, the players have 2 rounds to recapture it; otherwise, all players lose. If a town is captured and its associated main Hero is also defeated, the game ends immediately.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Timed Events}}
\textbf{Round 4:}
\begin{itemize}
\item A random Tile II--III adjacent to an Underground Tile is replaced with an Underground Tile IV--V. To select the Tile, you could drop a small die onto the map and replace the Tile closest to where the die lands. Alternatively, number the Tiles and roll a d4, d6, d8, or d10, depending on the number of Tiles, then replace the corresponding Tile.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Every following round, until all Tiles II--III adjacent to an original Underground Tile have been replaced:}
\begin{itemize}
\item A random Tile II--III adjacent to an Underground Tile is replaced with an Underground Tile IV--V.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Additional Rules}}
\begin{itemize}
\item The Starting (I) Dungeon Tile counts as an Underground Tile and cannot be entered until all Underground Gates have opened.
\item All players may trade Gold and Resources with another player once per round, or whenever they are on a Trading Post.
\item All players may trade Units, Artifacts, and Spells when their Heroes are on adjacent spaces.
\item When an Tile is replaced with an Underground Tile, all cubes are removed. Income is adjusted accordingly. The Underground Tile is unexplored unless a Hero is on that Tile.
\item If a Hero is on the Tile being replaced, they fall into the underground. The Tile counts as explored, but the weakest Unit takes damage: Pack \rightarrow Few, or Few \rightarrow dead.
\item As long as there is no path back to your home town, your underground Hero cannot recruit Units from their home town. However, if the Hero wins a battle against neutral Units, they can recruit one of the defeated Units into their army for half the cost.
\item As long as there is no path from your underground Hero back to your home town, you may recruit a second Hero for half the cost. All Units recruited in your town either remain in the town or join your secondary Hero.
\item The secondary Hero is Level I and receives the starting Units plus the Units recruited in the town (only those the main Hero does not have).
\item When the second Underground Tile becomes adjacent to your Starting Tile, an Underground Gate opens there, adjacent to your town. An enemy army appears there. At the very beginning of the next round, the enemy army attacks your town. You may also attack the army at the Underground Gate during the same round in which it appears.
\item The strength of the enemy army depends on the round in which it attacks or is attacked. The army consists of neutral Units from the Dungeon faction. Every two rounds, the number of Units increases by one. First, two \svg{bronze} Units (Harpy and Evil Eye) are added, then two \svg{silver} Units (Medusa and Minotaur), and then two \svg{golden} Units (Manticore and Black Dragon). For the attacking Hero, one random AI card with Attack or Defense is added every two rounds. (Example: In round 10, the army consists of the 2 \svg{bronze} Units, the 2 \svg{silver} Units, and 1 \svg{golden} Unit (Manticore). The AI deck contains 5 random Attack or Defense cards.)
\item If your main Hero is not in the town, you may pay 10 Gold to transfer your Units to your town. If your main Hero fell into the underground, you may instead defend your town with the Units recruited there (and possibly your secondary Hero). If the town is defended successfully, the way is clear and all surviving Units can be transferred to the main Hero. As a reward, the player receives 5 \svg{gold}, 1 \svg{artifact}, and \svg{morale_positive}.
\item If you lose the town, it no longer generates income and you can no longer recruit Units there. A player must recapture it within 2 rounds, or the scenario is lost.
\item After the last Underground Gate opens, the players have two more rounds to defeat the enemy Dungeon town. Otherwise, they lose.
\item The final battle takes place in the enemy Dungeon town (with walls and a Arrow Tower). The strength of the enemy army once again depends on the number of rounds. The army consists of Dungeon Units and is determined as follows:

Round 1 -- Few \svg{bronze} Troglodytes,

Round 2 -- Pack \svg{bronze} Troglodytes,

Round 3 -- Pack \svg{bronze} Troglodytes + Few \svg{bronze} Harpies,

Round 4 -- Pack \svg{bronze} Troglodytes + Pack \svg{bronze} Harpies, and so on.

\hspace{1em}\vdots

Round 14 -- All Dungeon Units on their Pack side.

To win, all Units must be defeated. If there are more than 5 Units, randomly select 5 and place them on the battlefield. Whenever one of them is defeated, another Unit is placed on the battlefield. The enemy Hero receives a number of AI bot cards according to the round, following this pattern: in rounds 1 and 2, one Spell card each; in round 3, an Ability card; in round 4, an Attack card; and in round 5, a Defense card. Then the pattern starts again: rounds 6 and 7 each get a Spell card, round 8 an Ability card, then Attack, Defense, two Spells, an Ability, and so on. The Ability is Sorcery, meaning the next Spell receives +2 \svg{empower}. Spells are selected randomly from the Earth Magic school (Stone Skin, Implosion, Sorrow, ...). If a player loses the battle, the defeated Units remain defeated, and the next player can continue the battle. The AI deck and Spells are shuffled again.
\end{itemize}
