\begin{multicols*}{2}

\textbf{Author:} dwarftoe

\textit{The earth is shaking! Oh no... it has begun here as well. Refugees from a distant kingdom had spoken of terrible earthquakes that had laid their entire homeland to waste. Whole regions had vanished beneath the trembling earth. Mountains had cracked, cities had collapsed, and the ground itself had been torn apart. Then, without warning, the earth split open. From the depths beneath the world, hordes of monstrous creatures poured forth. They swept across the land like a living tide, setting everything ablaze and storming the cities. No army had been prepared for such an enemy. No wall could withstand them. Kingdom after kingdom fell into ruin. Only a handful of people survived the devastation, fleeing as far as they could and carrying with them tales of the horror they had witnessed. But now... The same thing is happening here. The earth trembles beneath the feet of the people. Cracks are spreading across the ground. Something is stirring far below. The kingdoms have little time to act. For the first time, their rulers set aside their ancient rivalries and forge a desperate pact. Their armies gather, their gates are sealed, and their warriors prepare to face the unknown horror rising from beneath the earth. But there is one terrible question that none of them can answer: How do you fight an enemy you cannot see coming? The ground shakes again. Something is coming. Something ancient. Something hungry. Brace yourselves for the end.}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Scenario Length}}

5 + $2P$ Rounds

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Player Setup}}

\textbf{Player Count:} 1--5

\textbf{Starting Resources:} 13 \svg{gold}, 4 \svg{building_materials}, 1 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Income:} 10 \svg{gold}, 0 \svg{building_materials}, 0 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Units:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A ``Pack'' of the cheapest \svg{bronze} Units
  \item A ``Few'' of the most expensive \svg{bronze} Units
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Town Buildings:} \svg{bronze}~Dwelling, City Hall, \svg[12]{building_special_gears}~Building

\textbf{Map Tile Pool:} Each player takes 1 random Far (II--III) Map Tile

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Map Setup}}

Take the following Map Tiles and arrange them as shown in the Scenario map layout ($P$ stands for the number of players):
\begin{itemize}
  \item $P$ × Starting (I) Map Tiles
  \item 1 × Starting (I) Map Tile Dungeon
  \item $2P$ × Far (II--III) Map Tiles
  \item $2P$ × Near (IV--V) Map Tiles Underground
  \item 1-3 × Center (VI--VII) Map Tiles Underground (Depends on $P$)
  \item 1-2 × Near (IV--V) Map Tiles Underground (Depends on $P$)
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Victory Conditions}}
You have to go underground and defeat the evil Warmage in his town.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Defeat Conditions}}
At the end of round 5 + $2P$ the players have lost.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Timed Events}}

\textbf{\nth{4} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A random inner II-III-Tiles are excanged with an IV-V-Underground-Tile.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{every Round until all inner II-III-Tiles are excanged:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A random inner II-III-Tiles are excanged with an IV-V-Underground-Tile.
\end{itemize}


\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Additional Rules}}

\begin{itemize}
    \item If a II-III-Tile is exchanged with an underground tile, all cubes are removed. The income ist adjusted. The underground tile is unexplored except a Hero is on the tile.
    \item If a hero is on the swapped tile, they plummet into the underground. The tile counts as explored, but the units take damage. The weakest unit dies.
    \item As long there is no way to the home town, your underground hero cannot recruit units from his home town. But if the hero wins a fight, he can recruit one of the defeated units into his army for half the costs.
    \item As long there is no way from your underground Hero to the home town, you can recruit a second Hero for half the cost. All your in your town recruited units stay in town or with your second Hero.
    \item If the second underground tile is adjacend to your home tile an underground gate opens up. In the next round am enemy army attacks your town.
    \item The strength of the enemy army depends on number of the round it attacks. The units are neutral units from the Dungeon Party. Every second round the number of units rises of one. First two \svg{bronze}, then two \svg{silver}, then two \svg{golden}. For the attacking Hero, give them for every second round a random AI card with attack or defence. (For example in round ten: The army consists of 2 \svg{bronze} units, 2 two \svg{silver} units and 1 \svg{golden} unit. The AI bot cards counts 5 random attack or defence cards.)
    \item If your main Hero is stucked underground, you can pay ten Gold to transfere your Units to your Town. Or you defend your town with your units you recruited there.
    \item After the last Underground Gate opens up, the players have two more rounds to defeat the enemy town, otherwise they loose.
\end{itemize}
