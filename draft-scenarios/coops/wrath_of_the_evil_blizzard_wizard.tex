\begin{multicols*}{2}

\textbf{Author:} Dwarftoe

\textit{For countless years, an evil sorcerer had dwelled within his menacing tower on a forsaken island, far beyond the shores of three neighboring kingdoms. From the coastal villages, the people would often gaze toward the horizon and see a vast, black cloud hanging above the island, torn apart by flashes of lightning. From that cursed place came bitter winds that seemed to carry the chill of death itself. In their fear, many turned to an ancient sea god. His forgotten temple, half-swallowed by the waves, stood not far from the coast, its weathered stones rising from the dark waters like the ruins of a lost age. The people prayed that the old god might shield them from the sorcerer’s wrath. Yet none who dared sail toward the island ever returned. Every ship sent to confront the sorcerer met a terrible fate. Some were consumed by bolts of lightning that fell from the storm-wreathed sky, while others were seized by monstrous waves and hurled against the jagged cliffs surrounding the island. But then, without warning, everything changed. The winds began to howl with unnatural fury, and the cold grew deeper with every passing hour. A vast blizzard descended upon the kingdoms, sweeping across the land like an army of white shadows. Fields vanished beneath mountains of snow. Rivers froze solid. Forests became silent kingdoms of ice. Even the sea itself began to freeze. The people of the coast had no choice but to flee into their homes, barricading their doors against the howling storm. Those caught outside were soon lost to the merciless cold. And yet, one place remained untouched. Around the ancient temple of the sea god, the storm seemed to falter. The waters refused to freeze completely, and the winds weakened as they passed over the sacred ruins. Nearby stood a small, lonely island, where an old witch had lived in solitude for many years. Somehow, the witch and the ancient sea god together held the blizzard at bay, protecting the lands beyond from its icy grasp. But now, their strength was fading. The winds were beginning to break through. Ice crept farther and farther across the land, swallowing forests, fields, and villages one by one. The ancient protections that had endured for centuries were weakening, and soon even the temple and the witch's island might be consumed by the frost. The rulers of the three kingdoms now faced a terrible choice. There was little time left. They would have to set aside their rivalries, gather their armies, and venture into the frozen wastelands to confront the evil sorcerer at the source of his power. For if they failed, the blizzard would consume everything. Kingdoms would vanish beneath the ice. Ancient magic would be silenced. And all who called those lands home would be lost to an endless winter.}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Scenario Length}}

15 Rounds

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Player Setup}}

\textbf{Player Count:} 3

\textbf{Starting Resources:} 13 \svg{gold}, 4 \svg{building_materials}, 1 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Income:} 10 \svg{gold}, 0 \svg{building_materials}, 0 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Units:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A ``Pack'' of the cheapest \svg{bronze} Units
  \item A ``Few'' of the most expensive \svg{bronze} Units
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Town Buildings:} \svg{bronze}~Dwelling, City Hall, \svg[12]{building_special_gears}~Building

\textbf{Map Tile Pool:} Each player takes 1 random Far (II--III) Map Tile. Set aside all 4 Far (II--III) Map Tiles from Tower Expansion with ice background for later use.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Map Setup}}

Take the following Map Tiles and arrange them as shown in the Scenario map layout:
\begin{itemize}
  \item 3 × Starting (I) Map Tiles
  \item 1 × Starting (I) Map Tile Tower
  \item 6 × Far (II--III) Map Tiles
  \item 2 × Near (IV--V) Map Tiles with ice background
  \item 1 × Near (IV--V) Map Tile with elemtal wind background (counts as ice)
  \item 1 × Near (IV--V) Map Tile Water with witch hut in the middle
  \item 1 × Center (VI--VII) Map Tile Water with temple of the sea god
  \item 1 × Center (VI--VII) Map Tile Tower Settlement
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Victory Conditions}}
Defeat the evil wizard and conquer his town.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Defeat Conditions}}
At the end of round 15, everything is lost.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Timed Events}}

\textbf{\nth{3} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Exchange one random II-III-tile with an random ice-field (II-III-tile from tower expansion). For example, give the tiles numbers (1-6) and throw a dice. If you throw a 3, the third tile gets exchanged. All cubes on the tile are removed and the income is adjusted. 
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\nth{6} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Same as \nth{3} Round.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\nth{9} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Same as \nth{3} Round.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\nth{12} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Same as \nth{3} Round.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Additional Rules}}

\begin{itemize}
    \item All Players can exchange gold and resources once per round. They can also exchange units, artefacts and spells when their Heroes are on adjacent fields.
    \item When fighting on an ice field, all your units suffer 1 damage from the cold at the beginning.
    \item When your Hero is on a Far (II--III) Map Tile while it changes into an ice field, you got hit by the blizzard and your weakest unit dies.
    \item The first Hero who visits the temple of the sea god, gets his blessing. His units don't suffer 1 damage anymore when fighting on ice fields.
    \item The first Hero who visits the island with the which hut gets her help, an advanced ability card instead of a normal one and 3 upgrade crown tokens. You can keep them for yourself or share them with your comrades-in-arms.
    \item Before you can enter the town of the wizard, you have to defeat his garrison (the town on the VI-VII-tile next to the tower starting tile). The garrison consist of 2 \svg{azure} Titans, 1 \svg{golden} Naga, 1 \svg{silver} Genie, 1 \svg{silver} Mage (all neutral units) and town walls.
    \item The Player who defeated the garrison can choose between two options: (1) You can recruit or upgrade one of your \svg{bronze} or \svg{silver} Units for free, or one \svg{golden} Unit for half the costs. (2) You can recruit the \svg{silver} Genie or the \svg{silver} Mage for free, or 1 of the \svg{azure} Titans or the \svg{golden} Naga for half the costs.
    \item When you fight the wizard you have to defeat all 7 units of the Tower fraction on pack side. You start with random 5 and if one unit is defeated, another one enters the fight. The town has walls and the arrow tower. The wizard is an AI bot of Solmyr. You need 8 bot cards (1 Attack, 1 Defence, 2 Abillity, 4 Spells), the abillity is Sorcery, the Spells are: 2 × Lightning Chain, 1 × Lightning, 1 × Magic Arrow. 
    \item If a player loses the fight, the defeated units stay defeated (also walls and Arrow Tower), but the bot cards are reshuffled for the next fight.
\end{itemize}
